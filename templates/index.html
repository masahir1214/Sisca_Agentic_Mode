How to Use Sisca AI

  • Voice Command: Click Talk with SISCA and speak your command.
  • Gmail Integration: Connect your Gmail to let Sisca read, summarize, and send emails for you.
  • Image Analysis: Click the paperclip icon to upload an image and ask questions about it.
  • Web Access: Using Research Mode, you can browse the web and perform searches in real-time.
